Representative step-level timing guidance for the main computational stages is summarized below:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Steps 1--9: project initialization and workbook-to-YAML configuration typically require 30--60 min, depending mainly on how much library and experimental metadata must be entered and checked.
  \item Steps 10--13: sequence demultiplexing generally requires 10--30 min for small datasets, 1--2 h for medium datasets, and 2--6 h for large datasets.
  \item Step 14: launching and using the dashboard usually requires 15--60 min, depending on how extensively the count tables are explored.
  \item Step 15: selection-focused enumeration is expected to require less than 1 min across dataset classes.
  \item Step 16: selection-focused property calculation is expected to require less than 1 min across dataset classes.
  \item Steps 17--18: statistical-analysis setup and execution are expected to require less than 5 min across dataset classes.
  \item Step 19: optional full-library enumeration is expected to require less than 5 min for small datasets, 15 min to 2 h for medium datasets, and more than 2 h for large datasets, where execution may be impractical.
  \item Step 20: optional full-library property calculation is expected to require less than 5 min for small datasets, 15 min to 2 h for medium datasets, and more than 2 h for large datasets, where execution may be impractical.
  \item Step 21: optional molecular-representation generation is expected to require less than 5 min for small datasets, 15 min to 2 h for medium datasets, and more than 2 h for large datasets.
\end{itemize}

Notes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Parallel processing with the \texttt{num\_cores} parameter can substantially affect runtime
\end{itemize}
