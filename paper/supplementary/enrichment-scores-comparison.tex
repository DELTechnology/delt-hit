\subsection{Comparison of enrichment score rankings for the Favalli CA9 selection}
\label{supp:enrichment-scores-comparison}

To compare the ranking schemes implemented in DELT-Hit, we re-analysed the Favalli selection against CA9. As a
literature-supported positive-control motif, we focused on building block 1, code 474, which occurs in previously
reported CA9-binding compounds from related DEL selections\cite{ref37,ref38}. For each enrichment method
(count-based, edgeR, and z-score), compounds were ordered by the corresponding score, and the occurrence of
building block 1/code 474 was counted among the top 10, 25, 50, and 100 ranked compounds. This simple recovery
analysis provides a high-level comparison of how effectively the different ranking strategies prioritize a known
CA9-associated building block. The analysis indicates that the edgeR-based methods are more sensitive than pure
count-based ranking. Scripts to reproduce the analysis are described in \path{supporting_material/README.md}.
