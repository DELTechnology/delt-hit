\subsection{Advanced reaction-graph configurations}
\label{supp:reaction-graph-advanced}

In the single-stranded tutorial workflow used in the main protocol, the only additional reaction-graph step is an \texttt{SR} transformation from \texttt{product\_1} to \texttt{product\_1B}, represented in the \texttt{reaction\_graph} sheet by the row \texttt{product\_1, , SR, product\_1B}. More advanced library designs can require multiple precursor-activation paths even when they conceptually share the same transformation class.
